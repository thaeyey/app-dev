
# The Vampire Diaries

## About the Series

The Vampire Diaries is a supernatural drama series set in the fictional town of Mystic Falls. It follows the lives of Elena Gilbert, Stefan Salvatore, and Damon Salvatore as they experience love, friendship, secrets, and supernatural events.

## Main Characters

- **Elena Gilbert** - A young girl whose life changes after meeting two vampire brothers.
- **Stefan Salvatore** - A vampire who tries to live a good life.
- **Damon Salvatore** - Stefan's older brother, known for his sarcastic and unpredictable personality.
- **Bonnie Bennett** - A powerful witch and Elena's best friend.
- **Caroline Forbes** - Elena's best friend who eventually becomes a vampire.
